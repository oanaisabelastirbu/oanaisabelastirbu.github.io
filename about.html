My story

Curiosity across disciplines.

My academic journey began in Medical Engineering, where I earned both my bachelor's and master's degrees at POLITEHNICA Bucharest. I am currently pursuing a PhD in Electrical Engineering, focusing on the application of artificial intelligence and biomedical signal analysis to the study and assessment of human cognitive states.

My research interests and experience span biomedical signal processing, artificial intelligence, and machine learning, with a particular interest in EEG and other physiological signals. My work involves exploring computational approaches to signal analysis, feature extraction, and pattern recognition, with applications in understanding human attention, emotion, behavior, and mental health.

Alongside my academic and research activities, I have expanded my technical expertise through extensive professional training in Java backend and full-stack development. My software development background includes Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs, databases, and modern web technologies, complementing my engineering knowledge and strengthening my interdisciplinary approach to research and technology.
