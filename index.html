01 / About

A little about
my work.

My academic interests lie at the intersection of electroencephalography (EEG), computational methods, and psychological research. I am particularly interested in how signal analysis and artificial intelligence can contribute to the study of mental health and human cognition.

This website is a space to bring together my research interests, scientific publications, and academic projects.
